SEL0383 - Signals and Systems in Electrical Engineering

Objectives: To develop the theoretical foundations for the analysis and representation of continuous-time signals and systems. By the end of the course, students should be able to apply mathematical and computational tools to solve Electrical Engineering problems, develop analytical models, and integrate signal processing techniques into electrical and automation systems.

Workload: 60 hours (4 class credits)

Syllabus

1

Linear Algebra

Revision of basis, eigenvectors, and eigenvalues applied to systems.

2

Function Spaces

Introduction to scalar product, norm, and function representation.

3

Signals

Classification, properties, and special functions (impulse, step).

4

Convolution

Convolution operation and impulse response.

5

Fourier Series

Representation of periodic signals in the frequency domain.

6

Fourier Transform

Analysis of non-periodic signals and spectral properties.

7

Modulation and Filters

Amplitude Modulation (AM) and ideal filter theory.

8

LTI Systems

System classification, stability, and frequency representation.

9

Laplace Transform

Analysis in the s-plane, stability, poles, and zeros.
